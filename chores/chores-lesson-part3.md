# 🧠 English Lesson — Part 3

**Hey guys! It's your English-speaking friend, Kyle, and today we've got a video full of important vocabulary.**
I'm going to make you a video showing you something that we all have to do -- but I think  most of us don't really like doing: **chores.**

---

## ❓ What is this lesson about?

> In this lesson, you'll learn how to **introduce a topic naturally** in English videos or conversations.
> You'll also review expressions for talking about **things we all do**, even when we don't like them -- like *house chores!*

---

## 📖 Vocabulary

| 🗣️ Word / Phrase | 🇪🇸 Traducción | 📘 Explanation |
|------------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Hey guys!** | ¡Hola chicos! | Saludo informal muy común en videos o conversaciones casuales. Transmite cercanía. |
| **English-speaking friend** | Amigo que habla inglés | Literalmente, “tu amigo que habla inglés”. Se usa para sonar amistoso y natural. |
| **We’ve got** | Tenemos | Forma común y natural de decir “we have”. En habla rápida suena como *“we’ve got a video…”*. |
| **Full of important vocabulary** | Lleno de vocabulario importante | “Full of” = lleno de. Expresión útil para describir contenido o cosas. |
| **We all have to do** | Todos tenemos que hacer | Indica una obligación general o cotidiana. |
| **I think most of us don’t really like doing** | Creo que a la mayoría de nosotros no nos gusta hacerlo mucho | “Most of us” = la mayoría de nosotros. “Don’t really like doing” suena más natural que “don’t like to do” en contexto informal. |
| **Chores** | Quehaceres / tareas domésticas | Actividades del hogar: limpiar, lavar, cocinar, etc |

---

## 🔗 Connection & Fluency

To sound **smooth and natural`**, connect your ideas with short linking phrases:

> **And today...**, **So today...**, **Well...**, **Right?**, **Actually**, **Actually**

💡 **Example (natural version):**
> "Hey guys! It's your English-speaking friend, Kyle -- and today, well, we've got a video full of useful vocabulary."
> "I'm gonna show you something we all have to do, but, you kwon, most of us don't really like doing -- chores."

🧠 *Tips:*
- Use **“I’m gonna”** instead of “I’m going to” for informal, spoken English.
- Make a short **pause** before “chores” to emphasize it naturally.

---

## 💬 Practice & Personalization

🪄 Try adapting the paragraph with your own ideas:
- “Hey guys! It’s your Spanish-speaking friend, and today, we’ve got a video full of fun expressions.”
- “I’m gonna show you something that we all love — but don’t do enough — *reading!*”
- “Well, today, I’ve got something exciting for you — *let’s go!*”
