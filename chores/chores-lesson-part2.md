# 🧠 English Lesson — Part 2

**I'm going to make a video showing you all the chores - the chores that people do at their homes all around the world.**
But first, I have to **take out the trash**.
Let's go!

---

## ❓ What is this lesson about?

> In this lesson, you'll learn how to talk about **house chores** - the daily tasks people do at the home, like cleaning, washing, or taking out the trash.
> You'll also learn how to sound more natural using phrases like **"I'm gonna"** and **"Let's go!"**.

---

## 📖 Vocabulary

| 🗣️ Word / Phrase | 🇪🇸 Traducción | 📘 Explanation |
|------------------|----------------|----------------|
| **I'm going to make** | Voy a hacer | Frase natural para expresar planes inmediatos. En el habla rápida se dice **"I'm gonna make"** |
| **Showing you all the chores** | Mostrándote todos los quehaceres | “Chores” significa tareas o trabajos del hogar. |
| **All around the world** | En todo el mundo | Expresión común para hablar de algo que sucede globalmente. |
| **Take out the trash** | Sacar la basura | "Take out" -> Sacar algo de un lugar. Expresión típica del inglés cotidiano. |
| **Let's go** | ¡Vamos! | Muy usada para animar o comenzar una acción; da energía y naturalidad. |

---

## 🔗 Connection & Fluency

To sound **more conversational and fluent**, connect your ideas naturally using short phrases like:

> **Well**, **So**, **You know**, **Right?**, **Actually**, **I mean**, **Anyway**

💡 **Example (natural version):**
> "I'm going to make a video showing you all of the chores - you know, the things people do at home all around the world."

🧠 *Tip:* Adding fillers like *"you know"* or *"right?"* makes your speech sound relaxed and friendly - just like native speaker.

---

## 🗣️ Pronunciation Practice

🎧 Try repeating aloud:
- "I'm **gonna** make a video showing you all of the **chores**."
- "But first, I have to **take out the trash**."
- "**Let's go!**"


🎯 Focus on:
- Using **"gonna"** instead of **"going to"** for informal speech.
 Stressing **chores**, **trash** and **go** to emphasize the key actions.

---

## 💬 Practice & Personalization

🪄 Try saying your own version:
- "I'm gonna make a video showing you my favorite *recipes*."
- "I have to *wash the dishes* first.
- "Let's go and see what happens!"
