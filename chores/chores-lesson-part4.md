# 🧠 English Lesson — Part 4
## 🎬 Introduction

**What are chores?**
Chores are the things that you do around your house to keep it clean, to make sure that you have a nice place to live.

---

## ❓ What is this lesson about?

> In this lesson, you’ll learn how to **define everyday concepts** in English, like “chores”.
> You’ll also practice expressions used to **describe purpose** (why we do something) and **maintaining routines** at home.

---

## 📖 Vocabulary

| 🗣️ Word / Phrase | 🇪🇸 Traducción | 📘 Explanation |
|------------------|----------------|----------------|
| **What are chores?** | ¿Qué son los quehaceres? | “What are…” es la estructura general para pedir una definición o explicación. |
| **Things that you do around your house** | Cosas que haces alrededor de tu casa | “Around” significa “en diferentes partes de” o “en el entorno de”. |
| **To keep it clean** | Para mantenerla limpia | “Keep” = mantener o conservar algo en cierto estado. |
| **To make sure that…** | Para asegurarte de que… | Expresión muy común para indicar propósito o verificación. Ejemplo: “Make sure that you close the door.” |
| **A nice place to live** | Un lugar agradable para vivir | “Nice” se usa con frecuencia en el inglés cotidiano para expresar algo positivo o cómodo. |

---

## 🔗 Connection & Fluency

To make your speech sound **more natural and connected**, use soft transitions and fillers like:

> **So**, **Well**, **You know**, **Actually**, **Right?**

💡 **Fluent example:**
> “So, what are chores? Well, chores are the things you do around your house — you know, to keep it clean and make sure you have a nice place to live.”

🧠 *Fluency notes:*
- Starting a question with **“So”** makes it sound conversational, not robotic.
- **“You know”** softens the tone and helps keep listener engagement.
- In fast speech: *“to make sure that you”* → /tə mek ʃʊr ðətʃə/

---

## 🗣️ Pronunciation Practice

🎧 Try repeating aloud:
- “So, what are chores?”
- “Chores are the things you do around your house.”
- “To keep it clean and make sure you have a nice place to live.”

🎯 Focus on:
- Linking: *“around your house”* → /əˈraʊndʒər haʊs/
- Smooth rhythm and small pauses for clarity.
- Reducing “to” → /tə/ and “that you” → /ðətʃə/ in fast speech.

---

## 💬 Practice & Personalization

🪄 Try creating your own version:
- “So, what are hobbies? Well, hobbies are the things you do in your free time — you know, to relax and have fun.”
- “So, what is exercise? Exercise is what you do to keep your body strong and healthy.”
