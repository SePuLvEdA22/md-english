# 🏠 Lesson 2: Parts of the House and Prepositions **IN - ON - AT**

Welcome back! 🙌
In this lesson, you will learn **parts of the house** and how to use the prepositions **IN**, **ON**, and **AT** correctly.

---

## 📍 Prepositions of Place — **IN / ON / AT**

| Preposition | Meaning | Use | Example | Traducción |
|--------------|----------|------|----------|-------------|
| **IN** | dentro de | espacios cerrados o áreas grandes | William is **in** the house. | William está **en** la casa. |
| **ON** | sobre / encima de (contacto físico) | superficies | The book is **on** the table. | El libro está **sobre** la mesa. |
| **AT** | en (lugar específico o punto exacto) | direcciones o ubicaciones precisas | She is **at** the door. | Ella está **en** la puerta. |

🧠 **TIP:**
- Use **IN** for rooms, buildings, cities, and countries.
- Use **ON** for surfaces (table, wall, floor).
- Use **AT** for specific points (door, bus stop, address).

---

## 💬 Examples

1. William **is in** the house, **in** the living room, **on** the sofa.
   → William está en la casa, en la sala, sobre el sofá.
2. They **are in** the dining room, **on** the chairs, **at** the table.
   → Ellos están en el comedor, sobre las sillas, en la mesa.
3. The cat **is on** the bed.
   → El gato está sobre la cama.
4. My dad **is in** the garage.
   → Mi papá está en el garaje.
5. She **is at** the door.
   → Ella está en la puerta.

---

## 🧠 Grammar Focus

**Structure:**
👉 Subject + *to be* + Preposition + Place

**Examples:**
- I am **in** the kitchen.
- He is **on** the chair.
- They are **at** the door.

**Negative Form:**
- I’m **not in** the bathroom.
- He **isn’t on** the sofa.
- They **aren’t at** home.

**Question Form:**
- Are you **in** the bedroom?
- Is she **on** the bed?
- Are they **at** school?
