# 🧠 Lesson 1: Pronouns and the Verb **To Be**

Welcome to your first English lesson!
In this lesson, you will learn about **personal pronouns** and the **verb “to be”** (ser / estar).
Let’s start! 🚀

---

## 👤 Personal Pronouns

| English | Español | Example | Traducción |
|----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| I | Yo | I am Helger. | Yo soy Helger. |
| You | Tú / Usted | You are my friend. | Tú eres mi amigo. |
| He | Él | He is a teacher. | Él es profesor. |
| She | Ella | She is very kind. | Ella es muy amable. |
| It | Eso / Esa / Ese | It is a dog. | Es un perro. |
| We | Nosotros(as) | We are students. | Nosotros somos estudiantes. |
| They | Ellos(as) | They are happy. | Ellos están felices. |

🧩 **Remember:**
> *It* is used for animals, things or situations — not for people.
> Example: *It is raining.* → Está lloviendo.

---

## 🔤 Verb **TO BE** — *Ser / Estar*

| Subject | Verb | Example | Traducción |
|----------|------|----------|-------------|
| I | am | I am in class. | Estoy en clase. |
| You / We / They | are | You are my classmates. | Ustedes son mis compañeros. |
| He / She / It | is | She is my sister. | Ella es mi hermana. |

---

## ✏️ Contractions (Contracciones)

| Full Form | Contraction | Example | Traducción |
|------------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| I am | I'm | I'm a student. | Soy estudiante. |
| You are | You're | You're from Colombia. | Eres de Colombia. |
| He is | He's | He's at home. | Él está en casa. |
| She is | She's | She's happy. | Ella está feliz. |
| It is | It's | It's cold today. | Hace frío hoy. |
| We are | We're | We're ready. | Estamos listos. |
| They are | They're | They're friends. | Son amigos. |

---

## ❌ Negative Form

| Subject | Negative | Example | Traducción |
|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| I | am not (I'm not) | I'm not tired. | No estoy cansado. |
| You | are not (aren't) | You aren't late. | No llegas tarde. |
| He / She / It | is not (isn't) | He isn't here. | Él no está aquí. |
| We / They | are not (aren't) | They aren't students. | No son estudiantes. |

---

## ❓ Questions (Preguntas)

**Structure:** Verb *to be* + Subject + Complement + ?

| Example | Traducción |
|----------|-------------|
| Are you a student? | ¿Eres estudiante? |
| Is he your brother? | ¿Es él tu hermano? |
| Are they at home? | ¿Están ellos en casa? |
| Am I late? | ¿Estoy tarde? |

🧠 **TIP:** Always invert the order for questions.
Example:
✅ *You are happy.*
❌ *Are you happy?* ✅

- “I’m” sounds like **/aim/**
- “You’re” sounds like **/ju:r/**
- “They’re” sounds like **/ðer/**

## 🧠 Summary

| Concept | Key Idea |
|----------|-----------|
| **To be** | Means *ser* or *estar* |
| **Forms** | am, is, are |
| **Contractions** | I'm, You're, He's, etc. |
| **Negatives** | am not, isn't, aren't |
| **Questions** | Verb + Subject + Complement + ? |
